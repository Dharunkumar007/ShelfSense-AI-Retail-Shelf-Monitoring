Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect and Store

ShelfSense AI is a platform designed for retail inventory management. To provide our Service, we collect and store the following data:

  • Account Data: Usernames, roles, salted password hashes, and expiring session records.
  • Store Data: Saved scans containing compressed shelf images, filenames, source identifiers, timestamps, detection results, image-quality measurements, model identifiers, and inference settings.
  • Workspace Layouts: Shelf layouts, restock assignments, completion notes, and activity records.

2. Camera and Image Processing

Uploaded images and captured camera snapshots are sent to the workspace backend for model inference and storage. Starting a browser camera requires explicit permission. While Live Monitoring is active, snapshots are sent at the selected interval. Configured IP-camera snapshots are fetched securely by the backend. You should avoid including identifiable customers, staff, payment details, or unrelated personal information in shelf images.

3. Cookies, Storage, and Third-Party Providers

A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to eight hours, and your theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage. Our service worker caches application files for performance; it does not cache authenticated API responses or saved scan images. The application does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

The interface icon library is downloaded from unpkg.com, which receives the network information necessary to serve that request. Login throttling mechanisms temporarily use the connecting IP address in the server memory to prevent abuse.

4. Data Access and Retention

Authorized workspace accounts can access records according to their role. There is no automatic retention schedule enabled by default; records remain until the operator removes them. The optional retention command preserves scans referenced by restock tasks. Account and image deletion are not self-service features in this release; Workspace Administrators have the authority to execute a full factory reset to permanently delete active data.
