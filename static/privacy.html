Privacy Policy

Project edition / Updated 1 October 2026

About this workspace

ShelfSense AI analyzes retail shelf images. The person or organization operating your workspace controls its database, hosting, account access, and retention. This notice describes the current application; an operator may provide additional information for their deployment.

Information stored

The application stores account usernames, roles, salted password hashes, and expiring session records. Saved scans contain compressed shelf images, filenames, source identifiers, timestamps, detection results, image-quality measurements, model identifiers, and inference settings. Shelf layouts, restock assignments, completion notes, and activity records are also stored.

Camera and image processing

Uploaded images and captured camera snapshots are sent to the workspace backend for model inference and storage. Starting a browser camera requires permission. While monitoring is active, snapshots are sent at the selected interval. Configured IP-camera snapshots are fetched by the backend. Stop monitoring to end browser capture.

Cookies and browser storage

A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to eight hours. Your theme preference is saved in browser local storage. The service worker caches application files; it does not cache authenticated API responses or saved scan images. The application does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Access and service providers

Authorized workspace accounts can access records according to their role. The current application uses a shared workspace, not isolated customer accounts. Hosting and database providers may process records and connection logs. The icon library is downloaded from unpkg.com, which receives the network information needed to serve that request. Login throttling temporarily uses the connecting IP address in server memory.

Retention and requests

Records remain until the operator removes them. There is no automatic retention schedule enabled by default. The optional retention command preserves scans referenced by restock tasks. For questions, access requests, corrections, or removal requests, contact the person or organization that provided your workspace access. Account and image deletion are not self-service features in this release.

Responsible uploads

Use images you are authorized to process. Avoid including identifiable customers, staff, payment details, or unrelated personal information in shelf images and task notes.
